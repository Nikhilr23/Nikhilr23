## Style Prompt
Cinematic motion portfolio for a healthcare systems analyst and product builder: oversized editorial type, near-black canvas, electric lime signals, cool violet and blue project worlds, thin engineering lines, and motion that reveals hierarchy without slowing navigation.
## Colors
- #070806 canvas
- #EEF0E8 text
- #C9FF63 signal lime
- #9C8CFF project field
- #88B8FF project field
## Typography
- Manrope for headlines and body
- DM Mono for labels and system details
## What NOT to Do
- No generic gradient blobs
- No auto-playing audio
- No excessive glass cards
- No motion that blocks content
- No fake employment metrics
